# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Kunban is for people who work at a Windows desktop and want to check the next important commitment without opening a task manager. Its primary user is a single owner who needs an ordered, glanceable priority queue, then a fuller board when they intentionally engage with it.

## Product Purpose

Kunban is a desktop-native personal Kanban widget. It keeps urgent work visible, expands into planned and completed work on demand, and exposes a careful local integration surface so AI assistants and websites can request cards without being allowed to inspect a personal board.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, quietly premium. It should make a busy day feel legible rather than louder.

## Anti-references

Avoid noisy Trello-like grids, generic glass widgets, over-round cards, saturated status clutter, and attention-seeking task-manager chrome. The widget must not feel like a conventional maximizable application window.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next commitment before the whole system.
2. Reveal depth deliberately: a useful glance first, a board only on intent.
3. Make automation feel accountable and contained.
4. Respect desktop space and attention.
5. Use familiar controls with an unusually considered hierarchy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard operation, visible focus, high-contrast system themes, and reduced motion by default. Never rely on colour alone to convey priority or status.
